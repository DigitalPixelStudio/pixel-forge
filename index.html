Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Projects start from ₹25,000 for a single-page landing website to ₹5,00,000+ for complex web applications. The exact cost depends on features, complexity, and timeline. We provide a detailed quote after understanding your requirements.

How long does it take to complete?

A focused five-page website usually takes one to three weeks, depending on content readiness and feedback speed. Complex web apps take 6-10 weeks. Mobile apps take 8-12 weeks.

Is hosting and SSL included?

Yes. Hosting and SSL are included in our starter website plans for the stated term. Renewal costs and conditions are confirmed before work begins.

Is the website mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every website is designed and tested for mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens.

Do I get a CMS to manage content?

Choose a dynamic plan if you need a content-management system. We will also explain how to manage the agreed content areas.

Is SEO included?

Technical SEO foundations are included. Ongoing content creation, link building, and ranking campaigns are separate services.

Do I own the source code?

Ownership, source-code handover, hosting access, and third-party accounts are clearly defined in your proposal.
