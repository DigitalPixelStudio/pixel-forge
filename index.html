Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

How much does it cost to build a website?

Our projects start from ₹25,000 for basic landing pages to ₹5,00,000+ for complex web applications. The exact cost depends on features, complexity, and timeline. We provide a detailed quote after understanding your requirements. Contact us for a free consultation! 💬

How long does it take to complete a project?

Timelines vary by project scope: Landing pages: 1-2 weeks, Websites: 3-4 weeks, Web apps: 6-10 weeks, Mobile apps: 8-12 weeks. We follow agile methodology with weekly progress demos so you always know where things stand. ⚡

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?

Yes! We handle everything end-to-end. Hosting setup on AWS/Vercel/Netlify, SSL certificates, domain configuration, and ongoing maintenance packages. Our maintenance plans start at ₹5,000/month covering updates, security patches, and 24/7 monitoring. 🛡️

What technologies do you use?

We use modern, battle-tested tech: Frontend: React, Next.js, Vue.js, Tailwind CSS. Backend: Node.js, Python, Go. Mobile: Flutter, React Native. Cloud: AWS, GCP, Vercel. Database: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis. We pick the best stack for your specific needs.

Can you build a PWA (Progressive Web App)?

Absolutely! We specialize in PWAs. A PWA works like a native app — installable on any device, offline support, push notifications, and lightning-fast performance. PWAs combine the best of web and mobile apps at a fraction of the cost. 📱

Do you work with clients outside Bangalore?

Yes! While we're based in Bangalore, we work with clients globally. We use tools like Zoom, Slack, Notion, and GitHub for seamless collaboration. Many of our best projects have been delivered remotely to clients across India and internationally. 🌍

What is your payment structure?

We follow a milestone-based payment: 30% upfront to begin, 40% at midpoint (design approval), and 30% on delivery. For larger projects, we can arrange custom payment schedules. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and international wire transfers. 💳

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

100% yes! SEO is built into our development process: semantic HTML, meta tags & Open Graph, fast load speeds (90+ Lighthouse), mobile-first design, structured data, and clean URL structure. We also offer dedicated SEO consulting as an add-on service. 📈
